SlideWorks is the phone companion to SlideWorks web decks.

Join a talk: on the first screen, enter a six-digit code from a live talk. No account is needed.

Sign in: tap Sign in, keep the prefilled server, and tap Sign in with Google. Sign-in happens in the system browser. The demo account's login is in the App Review contact and sign-in fields set in App Store Connect, because this repository is public.

The app uses no camera, microphone or location. It uses the network only. Crash reports go to our crash service and carry no personal data.
