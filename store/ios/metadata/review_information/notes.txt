SlideWorks is the phone companion to SlideWorks web decks.

Test without an account: on the first screen, enter the six-digit code of a live talk (the demo talk's code is in the App Review notes field in App Store Connect) and tap Join a talk. The app shows the talk's title and the slide number the speaker is on. No account is needed to join.

Sign in (optional): tap Sign in, keep the prefilled server, and tap Sign in with Google. Sign-in happens in the system browser, through the server's web sign-in. The demo account's login is in the App Review contact and sign-in fields set in App Store Connect, because this repository is public.

The app uses no camera, microphone or location. It uses the network only. Crash reports are sent only in builds that have a crash-reporting DSN configured, and carry no personal data.
