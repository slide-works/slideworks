Profile page and sharing decks with people

Every screen SW-108 adds or changes, drawn in SlideWorks' own chrome. The share dialog in section 4 works: type in it. Purple pins mark what's new; the notes beside each screen name the contract or decision it follows. Section 8 lists the design questions and the answers given.

What changes for people

  • PProfile: set your name, colour and photo, pick a time zone, cut off agents.
  • SSharing: each deck is shared on its own, by full email every time. Each person gets Can view or Can edit on that deck and nothing else.
  • EEmail: a fixed "shared a deck with you" email, rate limited, with an unsubscribe page.

Built from C1–C9, D1–D24, Q1–Q3 on SW-108, and the current SharingDialog, UserMenu and admin list on main.

    8

    Design questions and the answers given

    Joel reviewed these mockups on 22 Nov 2026. D1–D11 were settled earlier that day. The build decisions (D13–D23) are on SW-108.

    Answered

    • Q2 · D12

      Does Private shut out people with Can edit too?

      Yes. At Private only the owner gets in. The people list is kept and shown as paused until the level changes back.

    • Q3 · D24

      Does a deck's people list show a person's name and avatar once they have an account?

      Yes. It tells the owner that the email has an account, capped by the limit of 10 new people an hour.

    • D25

      What do the two access levels say in the UI?

      "Can view" and "Can edit". The wire stays read and write.

    • D26

      Where does the people list sit in the share dialog?

      In its own People section under the three levels, because Can edit still works at "Anyone with the link". The third level is renamed "Only people below".

    • D27

      Does Disconnect on an agent ask first?

      Yes, with an inline "Disconnect Claude?" and Keep / Disconnect, because it can't be undone.

    • D28

      When does a photo upload take effect?

      Straight away: upload and remove are their own calls. Name and colour wait for Save.

    • D29

      Does the share dialog warn before the new-people limit?

      No. Nothing is shown until a share is refused, then it says when the limit resets.

    • D30

      Does the share email say view or edit?

      No. As C8 says: the sharer's name and email, the deck title, one link and the unsubscribe link.

    • D31

      Does the admin link move into the user menu?

      Yes, as "People (admin)" under Profile. The top-bar badge for people waiting stays.

    • D32

      Is there a favourites list that autocompletes people?

      No. Sharing is per deck and full email every time. No favourites, autocomplete or People you share with panel. This replaces D5 and D9.

    Open, going ahead with the recommendation

    • D33

      With no favourites, what counts against the per-sharer limit?

      Recommended: each distinct email newly added to any of the sharer's decks in the window. Adding one person to five decks counts once.

    • Q1

      Which SMTP provider and sender address does the hosted instance use?

      No screen depends on it. Until it's set, the hosted instance sends no share emails.
