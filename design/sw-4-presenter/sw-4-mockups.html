Presenting: the presenter window on the laptop, and speaker mode on the phone

A presenter runs a talk with speaker notes, the next slide, a timer, an overview and a deep-dive skip. They can do it from a second window on the laptop, or from their phone while the laptop drives the projector. Every mock on this page shares one presentation state: move a slide in any of them and the others follow, the way the real windows will.

What SW-4 covers now

Two changes from the Feature as written: the phone remote from SW-7 comes in, because presenting from the phone is the main way you'll run a talk, and PDF export goes.

In SW-4

  • Speaker notes per slide (set over MCP since SW-1), in the presenter window and on the phone
  • Presenter window: current slide, next slide or next reveal, notes, elapsed timer with pause and reset, optional talk length, clock, slide and step count
  • Audience window kept in step with the presenter window, both through DeckHub (D4)
  • Phone speaker mode: sign in on the phone, pick the deck you're presenting, then the current slide large with the notes underneath (D6), next and previous, next slide, timer, jump to slide, blackout and deep-dive skip
  • Overview grid of thumbnails grouped by part, click to jump
  • Deep dives (marked since SW-2): shown in the presenter view, and a "skip deep dives" toggle steps over them
  • Blackout and fullscreen

Not in SW-4

  • SW-173 export to PDF, one page per slide (filed at the back of the backlog, D2)
  • SW-5 audience joins by QR and follows along on their own phones
  • SW-6 poll controls on the phone (reveal results, close voting). Phone speaker mode keeps a slot for them.
  • Editing slides or notes from the phone
  • Recording or rehearsal timings per slide
01

How the three screens connect

One presentation state (slide, step, blackout, skip deep dives, timer). Every window and phone shares it through the existing DeckHub, in a new owner-only group (D4). There's one path to build and test. The cost: a Wi-Fi drop freezes the audience window until it reconnects (section 08). SW-4 doesn't wait for SW-5.

State, not commands.
Each move sends the whole state with a sequence number: {slide, step, black, skipDeep, timer}. A window that missed a message just applies the next one. Last write wins.
Who may join present:{id}.
The deck owner's signed-in sessions only (Q3). Link viewers and agents can't join it. People with the share link who open the deck aren't moved: following along is SW-5.
Notes never reach the audience.
The public player view model already leaves notes out (SW-1). Notes come only from the owner's deck API, so the audience window never has them.

Audience window on the projector (or the audience tab when there's one screen). While a presenter window or phone drives it, its control bar stays hidden and clicks on the slide do nothing, so a stray tap can't move the talk. Keys still work.

Presenter window rules

  • Opening it opens the audience window too. Closing the audience window shows "Audience window closed · Reopen".
  • The timer starts on the first move, not on open, so setup time doesn't count.
  • With a talk length set, the timer turns amber at 5 minutes left and red when over.
  • An agent's edit mid-talk (existing deckEvent) updates the slides in place and keeps the position.
  • Notes size is remembered per browser.

Overview

O in the presenter window · click a slide to jump

Thumbnails grouped by the deck's parts (the part tracker from SW-1), so "jump to the start of Ship it" is one click. Deep dives carry the pill, and dim when skipping is on. A jump always lands where you click, deep dive or not. The presenter only sees this: the audience keeps the current slide until you pick one.

05

Running late: skip deep dives

One toggle, on the laptop (D) and on the phone. While it's on, next and previous step over every slide marked deep dive. Nothing is removed from the deck, the audience sees no gaps in the count, and you can still jump into one from the overview if someone asks.

Skip deep dives is off
Count stays honest.
The counter keeps the deck's numbers, so it reads 9 → 11 → 13. Nothing renumbers, and the URL still says #11. The presenter view adds "2 deep dives ahead, skipping".
Steps on a deep dive don't matter.
A skipped deep dive is skipped whole, reveals included. Previous also skips it, so going back doesn't land you in one.
Part dividers.
SW-2's deepDivesOnDivider option lists a part's deep dives on its divider. When skipping, those names stay; the presenter just doesn't go there.

Questions for you

6 answered · D1–D6 on SW-4

Answered 2026-11-17 and recorded on SW-4 as D1–D6. Three went against my recommendation: PDF export is filed as SW-173 (D2), everything syncs through the hub (D4), and the phone shows the slide first (D6). SW-7 is closed as merged into SW-4. The forms still work if you want to change an answer.

Q1. What happens to SW-7 (phone remote)?

This page folds it into SW-4. SW-7 was waiting on SW-5's realtime channel, but the DeckHub that SW-5 would use is already built, so nothing blocks it.

Not queued

Q2. PDF export: drop it, or file it somewhere else?

It came from the catalogue: the hand-built decks had print CSS for handouts. The share link and the SW-2 share card already cover "send people the deck".

Not queued

Q3. Who can drive a presentation from a phone?

Decides who may join the present:{id} group. A wider rule makes a co-presented talk possible, and makes a hijack possible too.

Not queued

Q4. How do the presenter and audience windows on one laptop stay in step?

The phone has to go through the server. The two laptop windows don't.

Not queued

Q5. Does the timer know how long the talk is?

The deep-dive skip is for running late, and you can only see you're late against a target. The hand-built decks only count up.

Not queued

Q6. Phone speaker mode: notes first or slide first?

Mock 1 gives notes the most room and shows the slides as small thumbnails.

Not queued
