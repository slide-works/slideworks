Diagram building blocks, so diagrams aren't uploaded as pictures

Nine slides of the 44-slide test deck went in as pictures. This page settles which of them a built-in layout draws and which a custom layout draws, and shows the four built-in layouts and two inline blocks that result. Every slide below is drawn at 1920 × 1080 in the built-in Cobalt look, from the slots printed next to it. The build spec is gaps-c.md in this folder.

bars: a few named things compared by length

slide 16size M

2 to 6 horizontal bars with a label column. A bar can carry a note inside it, a dashed outline for what it was or was expected to be, a tone, and a short tag at its end. The numbers can be hidden when the lengths are illustrative.

Typical: slide 16 rebuilt
Slots
Ranked, values shown
The agent orders the list. A note that does not fit inside its bar is written after it.
Dark surface
One-by-one, after one click
One bar per click, label and bar together.
Accepted maximum (fit fixture barsSix)
Six bars with a label, a sub line, a value and a tag each, four notes, a legend and a caption. A label that wraps is refused at six bars.

Why not the chart layout

  • chart draws 1–3 series over 2–12 x labels of 12 characters. It is for a quantity over time or over categories.
  • bars is for 2–6 named things: a 30-character label with a sub line, text inside the bar, an outline, a tag.
  • They share nothing but rectangles, so chart stays as it is and gains no kind.
  • Values are never negative here. A chart with a zero line stays in chart.

04

levels and facts: a ladder with highlighted rungs, and a row of fact tiles

slides 9, 11, 13size M

Two inline blocks, like callout. The levels layout hosts both: the ladder on one side, and on the other a column of points, a paragraph or an image, with the tiles under it. A custom layout can place the same two blocks next to its own markup.

Typical: slide 11 without a custom template
Slots
Image in the main column: slide 9 for now
The mock-up is still an uploaded image. The ladder and the tiles are text, follow the palette and can be read aloud.
Dark surface, two levels highlighted
Ladder on the left, no tiles
Accepted maximum (fit fixture levelsSeven)
Seven rungs, two of them on two lines, a ladder title, a column title, three points and four tiles with two-line values.

The two blocks on their own

  • levels: an optional title and 2–7 rungs. Each rung has text, an optional mark of up to 3 characters (a number or a letter) and a state, plain or current. Any number of rungs can be current.
  • facts: 2–4 tiles. Each has a label, a value and an optional tone that colours its top rule.
  • Both are listed under "Inline blocks" in layouts.md and in list_layouts, beside callout, chip and badge.
  • No steps: the ladder is context, and the highlighted rung is the point.

Findings for the custom-layout wave, and hooks for groups A and B

No fourth binding is needed. The five custom layouts do need four things that D15 does not name. They are listed so the wave plans for them.

FindingNeeded byWhat to do
D15's three bindings
A number sets a CSS variableagent-lanes meters, dial needle, horizon segment widthsEnough as designed. The variable is a plain number, and calc() turns it into a percentage, an angle or a flex share.
A choice picks a variant classagent-lanes waiting row, chat-mock speaker, horizon segment fillEnough as designed.
data-stepagent-lanes, chat-mock messagesEnough as designed.
Not bindings, but needed
F1. Built-in blocks as slot typescode-mock, chat-mock, agent-lanesThe planned slot types name the callout, chip and badge blocks. Add levels and facts: a data-slot element whose slot is a block is filled by SlideWorks' own component.
F2. A monospace font tokencode-mock, agent-lanesThere is no --sw-font-mono, and font names are refused. Add one token, set by the template, with a system monospace stack as the built-in value. Noted on SW-50 as not yet a decision.
F3. A list inside a listhorizon (rows, then segments)data-each inside data-each, with data-var and data-variant reading the inner item. The mocked example has one level only.
F4. CSS the sanitiser must let throughdial, horizon, all meterscalc() on a bound variable inside width, flex-grow and transform: rotate(); conic-gradient and repeating-linear-gradient of tokens; mask with a gradient (no url()).
Hooks: marked here, designed by groups A and B
Callout (SW-357, group A)stack, bars, flow, levelsAll four are content layouts and take the callout slot wherever group A puts it. The fit rules here leave its height to a shared helper.
Icons (SW-361, group A)flow nodes, facts tilesAn optional icon field per node and per tile, drawn above the label. Not in the schemas in gaps-c.md until group A defines the type.
Inline emphasis (SW-360, group A)headlines, captionNothing particular to these layouts.
Step reveals (SW-364, group A)stack, bars, flowEach has reveal and a stepsFrom list, the same as bullets. If group A adds a per-item step, these lists take it, and slide 14's "one new segment on a click" becomes one click, not seven.
Dark surface (SW-359, group B)all four layouts, both blocksEvery colour is a token that has a night counterpart. The dark renders on this page show the mapping this design assumes.
