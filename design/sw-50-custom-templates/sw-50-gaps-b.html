From group A, assumed

  • Emphasis (SW-360) in statement.statement, quote.quote and a compare panel's line. Drawn here as an accent-coloured word.

  • Steps (SW-364): reveal: "one-by-one" walks a layout's ordered step targets. Group B names the targets.

Rules kept

  • A slide is a layout plus typed slots. Slot text is plain text.
  • Every colour is a --sw-* token. Dark slides use the night set.
  • Display text fits on the client (useFitText). Body text is checked on the server (SlideFit).
  • Hidden steps stay rendered (Reveal).

How to read the slides

  • A dashed outline marks a part that appears on a later step. The label gives the step.
  • A dashed square is an icon from group A.
  • "Maximum" slides hold the most text the server accepts for that arrangement.

SW-359 · A dark variant per slide

slide field surface

The source deck sets three quotes and one ownership slide on the dark surface for emphasis. The slide says so with one field. The layout does not change, and no slot is added. The word and its meaning match D16 for custom layouts: a layout declares light, dark or either, and with either the slide picks.

add_slide {
  "deck": "RzvK5rWa20ye",
  "layout": "bullets",
  "surface": "dark",
  "part": 6,
  "slots": { "kicker": "Ownership", "headline": "…", "items": ["…"],
    "callout": { "tone": "negative", "text": "…" } }
}

add_slide { "layout": "cards", "surface": "dark", … }
→ surface: the 'cards' layout is always light; statement,
  quote, bullets and big-number can be dark

add_slide { "layout": "title", "surface": "light", … }
→ surface: the 'title' layout is always dark

Left out, the slide takes the layout's own surface. list_layouts gives each layout surface and keeps dark (true only for the always-dark layouts).

How the tokens switch

On a dark slideresolves to
inknight-ink
mutednight-2 (body), night-muted (tracker, footer, source)
gridnight-line
card, chipnight-ink mixed 7% and 12% into night
accent-dark, the kicker, the statement's subaccent-glow
accent-light, good-soft, bad-softthe role colour mixed 22–24% into night, with night-ink text
solid panelsunchanged: panel-* and panel-*-ink

What this means for the build

  • One block of CSS on .sw-frame[data-dark='true'] re-points the light tokens. Layout components do not change.
  • The four always-dark layouts use only night tokens today, so the block does not touch them.
  • Tracker and footer are hidden by layout (title, divider, close, join), no longer by darkness.
  • Five new contrast pairs join the palette check, so a custom palette is held to them too.
  • Other layouts stay light until someone checks them dark and adds a baseline. Turning one on is a one-word change.

SW-358 · Statement layout

new layout statement

One sentence that carries the slide, light or dark, with an optional sub line. It is not close: it has a kicker, sits anywhere in the deck, keeps the tracker and footer, and its sub is a plain line, not a box. The sentence is display text: set at 210 px, shrinking to 110 px to keep three lines and the height that is left.

SlotKindLimits

revealall or one-by-oneone-by-one puts sub on step 1

Surface: either. Refused: statement.statement: too long to fit: it wraps to about 4 lines at the statement's smallest size, and at most 3 fit.

SW-362 · Cards that use the slide's height

cards, numbered-cards

Cards fill the body height. When every card is short the type is set large, by a fixed rule on character counts that the server and the client share, so nothing is measured in the browser. cards takes a fourth card and lays four out 2×2. A card can be highlighted (solid fill), carry a pin, or take its tone on a top rule (fill: "plain").

Maximum content

SlotChange
cards.cards1–4 items (was 1–3). Four are laid out 2×2
cards[].fillsoft, solid or plain (new). Plain keeps the card surface and draws an 8 px top rule in the tone's colour
cards[].pinnew, text, optional, 24. A small label in the card's bottom right corner
cards[].icongroup A's icon type (SW-361)
numbered-cards.cards[]gains tone, fill and pin, the same as cards

The scale rule

  • Large when every card's title is at most 32 characters, its text at most 70, and it has no chips. Otherwise regular, today's sizes.
  • Large: 50 px title and 30 px text in a row, 44 and 28 in the 2×2, 40 and 28 in four numbered columns.
  • CardScale in Core and cardScale.ts in the client hold the same rule. A shared fixture file keeps them the same.
  • The server then checks the tallest card against the height it gets: 620 px in a row, 296 px in the 2×2.

SW-363 · Compare panels: rows, a stamp and a large line

compare

A panel's body is exactly one of points (today), rows of label and value, or one quote-sized line. A panel can also carry a stamp, which can land on a step, and a footer pinned to its bottom edge. fill: "plain" draws the panel as a white card with the tone on its top edge and tag.

Panel slotKindLimits
pointslist of text1–4, each 90. Now optional: exactly one of points, rows, line
rowslist of {label, value}1–4; label 16, value 40
linetext100. Set at 40 px. Takes emphasis (A)
stampblock stamp16. A step target
footertext90
fillsoft, solid, plainplain is new
icongroup Abeside the tag

Notes

  • compare.reveal is new: one-by-one puts the stamps on step 1. With no stamp the slide has no steps.
  • Panels stay as tall as the taller one's content. They are not stretched to the body, so a short panel is not left half empty.
  • title stays required and stays at 50 characters. The long quoted sentence goes in line.
  • Existing slides have points and draw exactly as before.

SW-365 · A fuller quote

quote

The quote keeps its look and gains what the source slides had to push into the notes: a headline, a role line beside the name, a supporting stat, a follow-up line and a source. The quote is still display text and shrinks to the room the other parts leave, never below 36 px. The server refuses a slide where it would not fit at 36 px.

Surface: either. The stat takes the negative tone on both surfaces. The two-point timeline on slide 20 is left to the diagram blocks (group C).

SW-366 · Links slide

new layout links, new slot kind link

Up to six resources, each a kind, a linked title and a source line, with an optional QR code for one address. The QR code is the component the join slide already uses (src/qr/QrCode.tsx, error correction M, black on white whatever the palette). A link is its own slot kind, so the address is checked on the server and never parsed out of text.

SlotKindLimits
kicker, headlinetext40, 80 (headline required)
linkslist of object, required1–6
links[].kindtext, optional16, such as "Talk"
links[].titletext, required90. Drawn as the link
links[].sourcetext, optional100
links[].urllink, required500
qr{url, caption}, optionalurl link, 200. caption text, 60; left out, the address without its scheme

    "url": "https://talks.northwind.example/harness" }
],
"qr": { "url": "https://northwind.example/watch" }

  such as https://example.com/page
"url": "https://sam:secret@northwind.example"
→ links.links[0].url: must not hold a user name or password
"url": "northwind.example/watch"
→ links.links[0].url: must be an http or https address,
  such as https://example.com/page

A link is a whole address with the scheme http or https, a host, no user name or password, no spaces or control characters. The client checks the scheme again before it writes an href.

SW-368 · Image layout: no frame, image on the right

image

Two choices on the slide, not guessed from the file. frame: "none" draws the image straight on the slide for a transparent picture. position: "right" gives the image the right column at the full height, with the kicker, headline, points and callout on the left. Points take a bold lead. The callout is group A's slot; the small caption line stays for a real caption.

SlotKindLimits
framecard or none, optional, newdefault card: white backing, corners and shadow, as today
positionbelow or right, optional, newdefault below: headline on top, as today
pointslist of text or {lead, text}1–4; text 80; lead 30, text 80
captiontext, optional140, unchanged. Under the image on both positions
calloutgroup A's callout slotbelow: under the image and points. right: at the foot of the left column

Notes

  • Transparency is the author's choice. The server does not look inside the file.
  • With position: "right" the headline wraps in a 760 px column, so it may be at most three lines there (about 21 characters a line). The server says so.
  • The image is still never cropped and grows to at most twice its own size.
  • Existing slides have neither slot and draw exactly as before.
