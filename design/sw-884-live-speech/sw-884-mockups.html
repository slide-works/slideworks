Live speech V1: the speaker talks, every guest reads it in their language

A native speaker app does what speaker mode on mobile web does, plus one new button, Subtitles. The phone turns the speaker's voice into text on the device, the server translates each finished sentence once for every language a guest is reading in, and guests see two lines of subtitles under the slide. The speaker-mode layout is copied from SpeakerPage.tsx; the guest page and the language chip are SW-883's.

What V1 ships

under the Translations epic SW-942

V1 · SW-884 This Feature

  • A speaker app (Expo, iOS and Android) with everything speaker mode does on mobile web: the talk list, slide moves, notes, the timer, Slides, Skip deep dives, Blackout and Join code.
  • A Subtitles button in speaker mode's action row (D12), a one-line ticker of what was heard above Next (D13), and a sheet: start, pause ("off the record"), which languages to listen for, and the big-screen captions switch.
  • Speech to text on the phone, English and Thai at once for mixed talks: each line goes to whichever model is surer (D15). Models come from a server manifest (D6) on first use (D14).
  • Server translation of each final line through SW-943, once per language in use (D11).
  • A subtitle strip on the guest phone, in the guest's SW-883 language, with Hide.
  • Optional captions on the big screen, in the language heard, off by default per deck, toggled next to Join bar (D18).
  • Sign-in by address and Google, or a QR code from the laptop (D21).
  • Model ids and latency on every line, so models can be compared with friends in production.

V2 · SW-885 Listening

  • A Listen button on mobile web (the phone's own voices).
  • Audience mode in the app: audio with the screen locked, headphones, voice choice.
  • A cloud speech-to-text engine as a fallback.
  • The lapel-mic tip in the setup help.

elsewhere Not in V1

  • Multilingual typed Q&A: V3, SW-944.
  • The translation engine, its models and the per-pair admin: SW-943.
  • Audio is never stored or uploaded. The transcript is kept only when the speaker turns it on (D4).
  • Listening in the background: leaving the app or locking the phone pauses subtitles (D16).

  sttModel: "parakeet-tdt-0.6b-en@1", confidence: 0.91 })

// DeckHub → audience-slide:{deck}:th (SW-883's group)

  text: "การเปลี่ยนแปลงที่เล็กลงได้รับการรีวิวภายในวันเดียวกัน",
