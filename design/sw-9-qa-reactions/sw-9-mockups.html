Q&A and reactions: the quiet majority gets a say

The guest page from SW-5 gets a second tab, Questions, where anyone can ask and upvote for the whole session, and a row of five reactions under the slide. The presenter gets a Questions panel next to the notes: approve, hide, mark answered, and put one question on the big screen. This page mocks the guest phone, the big screen and the presenter window. The live demo runs a session, and you can ask, upvote and react on the phone yourself. The nine questions at the end were answered on 17 Nov (D1–D9).

01

What SW-9 ships

In SW-9

  • A Questions tab on the guest phone, open for the whole session: ask, upvote, see what's been answered
  • Asking in a sheet: up to 200 characters, anonymous unless the guest adds a name (D3)
  • Upvotes, one per guest per question, no downvotes. The list sorts by votes or by newest
  • A Questions panel in the SW-4 presenter window and a strip on the phone remote: approve, hide, mark answered, show on screen
  • Show on screen: one question as a card over whatever slide is up, and a qa slide layout that lists the top questions (D5)
  • Reactions: five emoji under the slide on the phone, floating up the edge of the big screen for a couple of seconds
  • Deck settings for questions, review-first, names, reactions and where a shown question appears, with the same controls in the presenter panel. Questions and reactions are on by default and can be turned off for the deck or for one slide (D2)
  • MCP tools so an agent can turn Q&A on, add the Q&A slide and read the questions afterwards

Not in SW-9

  • SW-8 word clouds and open text, including the profanity filter. SW-9 moderation is the presenter's own eyes
  • SW-11 nicknames that persist across activities, scores and leaderboards
  • SW-12 exporting questions to a file, comparing sessions
  • later downvotes, replies to a question, merging duplicates, a second moderator who isn't presenting
  • later custom emoji sets and reaction totals per slide in the results view

On the big screen

Nothing from the audience reaches the big screen unless the presenter put it there, with one exception: reactions, which are five fixed emoji and carry no text. A question appears either as a card over the current slide or on a qa slide the deck's author placed, usually last.

Presenter controls

A question has four states the room can be in, and the presenter moves it between them. The controls sit in the Questions panel of the presenter window and in a strip on the phone remote. They work on any slide, because questions arrive on any slide.

06

The Q&A model

The Feature says to build on SW-6's poll model: a question is an option the audience submitted, and an upvote is an answer with downvotes off. That mapping is below, with the three places where SW-6's contracts don't fit yet. Moderation state belongs to SlideWorks and sits beside the poll, not in it. D8 settles it: Q&A is a real poll, and SW-6's contracts change before it is built.

How Q&A maps onto the poll model
The Q&A of one live session=one poll round, open from Go live to End A question=an option the audience appended (add_poll_option already appends while open) A guest's upvotes=one answer row: picks is the questions upvoted, replaced whole with a revision Vote counts=the tally, pushed at most twice a second Downvotes=off: the method is "pick any" Approve, hide, answered, on screen≠SlideWorks' own Question row per option. The poll never knows
Where SW-6 has to give
  • A poll belongs to a slide. Q&A belongs to the deck and runs on every slide, so it needs a poll that isn't keyed by one.
  • One open round per session. A Q&A round is open all session, next to whichever slide poll is running. The rule becomes one open round per session per kind.
  • Options are slide slots, 2 to 8 of them, 60 characters. Questions are rows, up to 200 characters, with no upper count, an author and a time.

SW-6 isn't built yet (plan captured, wave 0 not started). D8: these three go into its contracts now, as an amendment to its plan, not a retrofit later. SW-8's word cloud needs the same audience-written options.

Rules
  • One upvote per guest per question. Tapping again takes it back. Asking a question upvotes it for you, so it starts at 1.
  • A question can't be edited once sent. The asker can't delete it either; the presenter can hide it.
  • Hidden means hidden. A hidden or waiting question never reaches another guest's browser, counts included.
  • Answered freezes the votes. It stays readable on phones for the rest of the session.
  • Each Go live is a clean slate. Questions belong to the session (SW-5 D4). Earlier sessions stay readable by the presenter and by agents.
  • Nothing is deleted by the app. Hide and Answered are states.
  • Late joiners get the current list at once, in its current order.
Privacy
  • Anonymous means anonymous to everyone. A question is stored against the random guest ID from SW-5, which has no name. No screen or tool shows which guest asked or upvoted, to the presenter or anyone else.
  • A name is whatever the guest typed. It isn't checked, so the presenter panel shows it as given, and Hide is the answer to a rude one.
  • Reactions are counted, not kept per person. The server stores totals per slide and whether a guest reacted at all in the session, which the measure needs.
Measure (D9)
  • Questions asked per session, and the share of joined guests who asked, upvoted or reacted at least once.

08

Realtime and limits

Everything rides on what SW-5 and SW-6 put in place: guests write over plain HTTP with the sw_guest cookie, and changes come down the audience channel at most twice a second. The new part is reactions, which are high-volume and worthless a few seconds later, so they are counted in memory, batched and never fanned out to phones.

MessageCarries
audienceState.roomHub → guests. { questions, names, reactions, onScreen }. Tells a phone which tabs and rows to draw, already worked out for the slide on screen. Separate from activity, so a poll and Q&A run side by side.
GET …/questionsGuest → server. The questions this guest may see (open, answered, and their own in any state), their own upvotes and a revision. Read on load, on reconnect and when told the list changed.
POST …/questionsGuest → server. { text, name? }. 201 with the question, 400 naming the rule, 409 when questions are closed, 429 past the limit.
PUT …/questions/votesGuest → server. { picks, revision }, SW-6's answer shape: the whole set of upvoted questions, replaced.
questionTallyHub → guests and presenter. { counts, listRevision }, at most twice a second. A changed listRevision means re-read the list.
POST …/reactionsGuest → server. { emoji }, one of five keys. 204, or 429 past the limit.
reactionsHub → big screen and presenter only. { counts } since the last batch, up to four batches a second.
approve · hide · answered · show · settingsPresenter → server, over HTTP, with SW-4's can-present check.
Limits
  • A question: 1 to 200 characters after trimming, plain text, one line
  • A name: up to 30 characters
  • Asking: 3 questions a minute per guest, then a wait with a countdown
  • Upvoting: SW-6's 30 answers a minute per guest (D18)
  • Reacting: 10 taps in 5 seconds per guest; the button greys out briefly past that
  • On the big screen: at most 30 emoji at once, the rest folded into "+N"
  • The Q&A slide: five rows, one line each. Longer questions are cut with an ellipsis and shown whole when put on screen

Load: SW-6's harness gets one more script: 1,000 guests who each upvote three questions and tap ten reactions inside 30 seconds, held to SW-6's gates (write p95 under 1 s, on screen p95 under 2 s, errors under 1%).

Q1 · Where does Q&A live on the phone?

Q&A runs all session, but SW-5's page has one activity slot and SW-6's polls take it over.

Not queued

Q2 · Are Q&A and reactions on for every live session?

Agents build most decks. A presenter who didn't ask for Q&A shouldn't find 20 unread questions afterwards, or emoji drifting over a board update.

Not queued

Q3 · Can an asker give a name?

The Feature says "optionally anonymous". SW-5 gave guests no names (D2) and SW-6 left named answers to the quiz Feature (D5).

Not queued

Q4 · Does the room see a question before the presenter has?

The Feature lists "approve / hide". Approving every question is safe and slow; a presenter mid-talk can't read a queue.

Not queued

Q5 · How do questions get onto the big screen?

SW-6 made a poll a slide. Questions arrive on every slide, so a slide alone doesn't cover "show the top question on screen".

Not queued

Q6 · Which reactions, and who sees them?

The Feature says "emoji taps on any slide, shown briefly on the big screen".

A poll is open for maybe thirty seconds. A guest on the other tab could miss it, or be halfway through typing a question.
