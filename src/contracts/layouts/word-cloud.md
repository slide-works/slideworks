## word-cloud

Word cloud slide: a question the room answers on their phones with up to 3 words each, while the deck is live. Words that several people send grow bigger. The words come from the live session, never from the slots.

| Slot | Kind | Required | Limits | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `question` | text | yes | up to 120 chars |  |
