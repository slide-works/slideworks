## word-cloud

Word cloud slide: a question the room answers on their phones with up to 3 words each, while the deck is live. Words that several people send grow bigger, and a word one typo from another counts with it. The words come from the live session, never from the slots.

| Slot | Kind | Required | Limits | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `question` | text | yes | up to 120 chars |  |
| `turn` | `none` or `some` | no | default `none` | 'none' (the default) keeps every word level; 'some' turns about one word in three on its side |
